Imagine you met a native speaker who said: "text or call me anytime you want to practice German."

Youtionary is an iOS app with an AI you can ask questions and have real voice calls with. It quietly notes what you're still figuring out, then generates Duolingo-style practice questions, powered by spaced repetition, so your sessions stay relevant to you.

Supported languages: German, French, Italian, Spanish.

Website: https://youtionary.com

Download it on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/youtionary-ai-language-friend/id6756575480).
