Moved Show was a YouTube channel about real expat stories in Europe.

When I [went solo in 2025](/annual-review-2025/), this was the first idea I pursued seriously. I interviewed friends in Berlin about how they ended up in Europe, what changed after moving, and what life away from home was really like.

I recorded more than 10 interviews and published 12 long-form videos on YouTube.

The project taught me a lot, especially about the brutal economics of video production. Editing a one-hour conversation down into a watchable 20-minute episode could take anywhere from 8 to 20 hours. I enjoyed the conversations, but the production workflow was too slow and draining to sustain.

That pain directly led to [my next idea: TrimScribe](/i-now-have-a-product-50-users-and-zero-certainty/), a tool for editing videos through the transcript instead of the timeline.

Moved Show did not become the media business I imagined, but it did what early projects often do: it exposed a real problem, forced me to learn by doing, and pointed me toward the next thing.
