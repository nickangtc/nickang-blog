## What this is

An experiment to grow an Instagram account from 0->1k followers in a month. The stats in the end:

- 4 viral posts above 100k views
- many more posts above 10k views
- 370 followers

## Why I built it

I wanted to learn how to ride a social media platform's [algorithm](/how-instagram-became-my-unexpected-growth-engine/) to get huge number of views.

I picked Instagram, followed my wife's [playbook](/when-success-comes-fast-you-know-its-right/) (0->10k followers in 2 months), and published 2-3 reels everyday for a month.

It didn't matter to me what the topic was as long as I saw myself being able to produce content for it for a month. The real goal was to exercise my marketing muscles.
